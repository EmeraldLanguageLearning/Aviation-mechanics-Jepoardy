# Aircraft Mechanics Jeopardy

A Jeopardy-style English game for aircraft mechanics students (light training aircraft), styled for Emerald Language Learning and made for iPad.

- `index.html`: menu
- `players.html`: 6 individual players
- `teams.html`: 2 teams

Each game has 30 clues in 6 categories, an Aircraft Recognition row (close-ups that zoom out to the whole aircraft after 10 seconds), a Daily Double and a Final round. Opened from the trainer area of the Emerald website.
